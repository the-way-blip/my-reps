3. DESCRIPTION (4000 char max)
------------------------------
Plan your Michigan ballot before election day. Of For & By The People helps you walk into the polls confident you're voting your values.

HOW IT WORKS
Enter your Michigan address and instantly see your voting districts — U.S. House, State Senate, and State House. Then explore every race on your November ballot with candidates graded A through F on six core conservative values.

BALLOT PLANNER
See every race you'll vote on. Compare candidates side by side, view their values alignment grades, and lock in your picks. Bring your completed ballot plan to the polls — no more guessing in the booth.

VALUES ALIGNMENT GRADES
Every candidate is graded A through F on six issues:
- Protection of Unborn Life
- Religious Liberty
- Second Amendment
- Marriage & Family
- Limited Government
- Fiscal Responsibility

Grades are based on voting records, public statements, and scorecards from 30+ conservative organizations. Our methodology is fully transparent and available in the app.

VOTER RESOURCES
- Find your polling place via Michigan Secretary of State
- Check your voter registration status
- Request an absentee ballot
- View your official sample ballot
- Key election dates and deadlines

YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL
- Download all your data as JSON at any time
- Permanently delete your account and all associated data from within the app
- No data sold to third parties

Of For & By The People is independent — not paid for by any candidate or party. We built this for conservative voters who want to vote informed without hours of research.

Currently covering Michigan for the November 2026 general election. More states coming soon.
