3. DESCRIPTION (4000 char max)
------------------------------
Do you know who represents you? From your local city council to the U.S. Senate, MyReps puts every elected official at your fingertips — along with the tools you need to hold them accountable.

MyReps is the civic engagement app built for Americans who take their constitutional rights seriously. Whether you're preparing for Election Day, tracking how your representatives vote on the issues that matter most, or looking up who represents your district, MyReps makes it simple.

FIND YOUR REPRESENTATIVES
Enter your address and instantly see every elected official who represents you — federal, state, and local. View their contact information, office addresses, committee assignments, and more. Works for all 50 states.

CONSTITUTIONAL ALIGNMENT SCORECARD
How does your representative measure up? Our exclusive scorecard grades every legislator (A through F) on six core constitutional issues:
- Pro-Life
- Traditional Marriage
- Religious Liberty
- Second Amendment
- Limited Government
- Fiscal Responsibility

INTERACTIVE DISTRICT MAPS
Explore your congressional and state legislative districts with full interactive maps. See exactly where district boundaries fall and who represents each area.

CONTACT YOUR REPRESENTATIVES
Make your voice heard. Call, email, or write letters to your representatives directly from the app. Advocacy has never been easier.

ELECTION CENTER
Stay informed with upcoming election dates, candidate information, and polling locations. Add election dates directly to your calendar so you never miss a vote.

MY BALLOT PLANNER
Research candidates and plan your vote before Election Day. Walk into the polling place confident and prepared with your personalized ballot plan.

COMMUNITY PULSE
See how other engaged citizens in your area are planning to vote. Get a pulse on community sentiment and stay connected with like-minded voters.

VOTER REGISTRATION
Check your voter registration status instantly. MyReps provides direct links to your state's official registration portal for all 50 states. No excuses — make sure you're registered.

CAMPAIGN FINANCE
Follow the money. View campaign finance data sourced directly from the Federal Election Commission (FEC) so you know who funds your representatives.

FOUNDING DOCUMENTS LIBRARY
Carry the Constitution, Bill of Rights, all 27 Amendments, and timeless quotes from the Founding Fathers in your pocket. The principles that built this nation, always accessible.

BUILT FOR YOUR LIFESTYLE
- Dark Mode for comfortable reading day or night
- Offline Support so you have access even without a connection
- Cloud Sync keeps your profile and preferences across all your devices
- Clean, intuitive design that makes civic engagement effortless

MyReps was built with a heart for Michigan but works nationwide. Whether you live in Detroit or Dallas, rural Montana or downtown Miami, you deserve to know who represents you and how they're voting.

Your representatives work for you. It's time to start holding them accountable. Download MyReps today and become the informed, engaged citizen our republic needs.
